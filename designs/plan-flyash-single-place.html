Flyash — one place for every Flyash bill (plan)

Same page on both websites and in the owner app. Nothing is built yet.

Today vs. after

Today — 4 screens, 2 steps • Infra → FlyAsh tab: Flyash bills made in Infra → Move (waiting)
• B&B → Infra Orders tab: Infra bills (view) · Bills to accept → Accept / Send back · Old bills to Infra
• B&B → Bills: bills made in B&B itself (not on the Infra screens)
• A moved bill becomes one B&B bill per item and loses its discount
• Nobody sees all Flyash bills together, and a move needs two people/steps
After — 1 screen, 1 step • Flyash Bills: every Flyash bill from both businesses in one list
• Each bill shows where it is now: Infra B&B or To decide
• Owner/Admin picks Infra or B&B — it moves at once (no accept step), either direction
• A moved bill stays one bill with all its items, discount, place and notes; cash on delivery moves with it; payments already received stay where they were
• Old Move / Accept / Send back / Old bills screens are removed (Raja / Ragupathi tabs stay until settled)

Which bills are listed
• Every bill of RRP Bricks & Blocks.
• Every RRP Infra bill for a Flyash product (the products linked to a Bricks & Blocks product, e.g. "Flyash Bricks").

"To decide"
• New Flyash bills made in Infra (from Infra orders) stay in Infra marked To decide until the owner picks. Bills waiting in today's Move/Accept are shown as To decide on go-live.

Who
• Everyone (except drivers) sees the list; only Owner / Admin can move bills.

Not allowed to move (shown with the reason)
• Bill sent in a GST bill, cancelled, or with a payment from Payment In applied (remove it first) — same rules as today.
